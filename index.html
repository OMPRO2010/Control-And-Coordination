01 • THE CORE IDEA

Sense. Process. Respond.

Control and coordination allow organisms to respond appropriately to changes in their surroundings.

👁️

DETECT

Receptors detect stimuli such as light, heat and pressure.

🧠

PROCESS

The nervous system processes information and coordinates responses.

⚡

RESPOND

Muscles and glands produce appropriate responses.

02 • CONTROL CENTRE

Meet the Brain.

The brain is the main control centre of the nervous system.

01 Forebrain — thinking, reasoning and memory.
02 Midbrain — helps coordinate some visual and auditory responses.
03 Hindbrain — balance, posture and involuntary actions.
03 • QUICK RESPONSE

Reflex Action & Reflex Arc

A reflex action is a quick, automatic and involuntary response to a stimulus.

⚡

REFLEX ACTION

A rapid and automatic response to a stimulus.

EXAMPLE Pulling your hand away from a hot object.
PURPOSE Helps protect the body.
🧠

REFLEX ARC

The pathway followed by the nerve impulse during a reflex action.

01 Receptor
02 Sensory neuron
03 Spinal cord
04 Motor neuron
05 Effector

04 • PLANT COORDINATION

Plants respond without a brain.

Plants use hormones and growth movements to respond to changes in their surroundings.

☀️

PHOTOTROPISM

Growth response to light.

🌍

GEOTROPISM

Growth response to gravity.

💧

HYDROTROPISM

Growth of roots towards water.
