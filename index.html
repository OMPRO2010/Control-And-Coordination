01 • THE CORE IDEA

Sense. Process. Respond.

Control and coordination allow organisms to respond to changes around them.

👁️

DETECT

Receptors detect stimuli such as light, heat, pressure and chemicals.

🧠

PROCESS

The nervous system processes information and coordinates responses.

⚡

RESPOND

Muscles, glands and plant parts produce suitable responses.

02 • THE CONTROL CENTRE

Meet the Brain.

The brain coordinates many activities of the body.

01 Forebrain — thinking, memory and voluntary actions.
02 Midbrain — helps coordinate certain visual and auditory reflexes.
03 Hindbrain — balance, posture and involuntary actions.
