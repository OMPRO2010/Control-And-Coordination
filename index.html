01 • THE CORE IDEA

Sense. Process. Respond.

Control and coordination allow organisms to respond to changes around them.

👁️

DETECT

Receptors detect stimuli such as light, heat and pressure.

🧠

PROCESS

The nervous system processes information.

⚡

RESPOND

Muscles and glands produce appropriate responses.

02 • CONTROL CENTRE

Meet the Brain.

The brain is the main control centre of the nervous system.

01 Forebrain — thinking and memory.
02 Midbrain — coordinates some visual and auditory responses.
03 Hindbrain — balance and involuntary actions.
03 • QUICK RESPONSE

Reflex Action & Reflex Arc

A reflex action is a quick, automatic and involuntary response to a stimulus.

⚡

REFLEX ACTION

A rapid and automatic response to a stimulus.

EXAMPLE Pulling your hand away from a hot object.
PURPOSE Helps protect the body.
🧠

REFLEX ARC

The pathway followed by a nerve impulse during a reflex action.

01 Receptor
02 Sensory neuron
03 Spinal cord
04 Motor neuron
05 Effector
