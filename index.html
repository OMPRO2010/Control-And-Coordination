01 • THE CORE IDEA

Sense. Process. Respond.

Control and coordination allow organisms to respond appropriately to changes in their surroundings.

👁️

DETECT

Receptors detect stimuli such as light, heat, pressure and chemicals.

🧠

PROCESS

The nervous system processes information and coordinates responses.

⚡

RESPOND

Muscles, glands and plant parts produce suitable responses.

02 • THE CONTROL CENTRE

Meet the Brain.

The brain is the main control centre of the nervous system.

01 Forebrain — thinking, memory, reasoning and voluntary actions.
02 Midbrain — helps coordinate certain visual and auditory reflexes.
03 Hindbrain — balance, posture and involuntary actions.
03 • QUICK RESPONSE

Reflex Action & Reflex Arc

A reflex action is a quick, automatic and involuntary response to a stimulus. The pathway followed by the nerve impulse is called the reflex arc.

⚡

REFLEX ACTION

A rapid, automatic and involuntary response to a stimulus.

EXAMPLE Pulling your hand away from a hot object.
PURPOSE It helps protect the body from injury.
🧠

REFLEX ARC

The pathway through which a nerve impulse travels during a reflex action.

01 Receptor detects stimulus.
02 Sensory neuron carries the impulse.
03 Spinal cord processes the information.
04 Motor neuron carries the instruction.
05 Effector produces the response.
